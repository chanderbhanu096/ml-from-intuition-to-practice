## What this module is for

Time series. Every idea from module 04 returns with a harder constraint: the future must not leak into the past, in the features, in the split, or in the evaluation.

Classical methods and machine-learning methods both appear, and the baselines in 09-04 are genuinely hard to beat.

## Before you start

- **Needs:** 04-01, 04-05, 05-11
- **Unlocks:** 12-02, 12-04, 14-01
- **If you are short of time:** **09-01** (framing), **09-03** (lags) and **09-05** (backtesting) are the ones that prevent silent disasters. 09-07 (ARIMA) is marked optional.
