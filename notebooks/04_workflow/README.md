## What this module is for

The spine of the course. Framing, baselines, splitting, leakage, preprocessing, pipelines and reproducibility - the part that separates a working model from an impressive-looking one.

Nine of the later modules name a chapter from here as a prerequisite. If you skip anything in this course, do not skip this module.
